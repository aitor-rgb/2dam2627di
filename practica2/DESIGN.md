## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an intimate, ultra-premium nightlife atmosphere tailored for exclusive jazz lounges, private salons, and high-end hospitality venues. It balances the timeless opulence of art deco jazz heritage with the precision and speed required for real-time guest capacity tracking, VIP table allocation, and door management.

### Personality & Tone
- **Atmospheric & Intimate:** Immersive darkness evocative of low-lit velour banquettes, smoke-tinted crystal, and polished brass fixtures.
- **Prestige & Discriminating:** Refined details, hairline metallic borders, and radiant accents that convey bespoke service rather than utilitarian security.
- **Decisive & Effortless:** Clean operational telemetry engineered for low-light legibility and instant high-stress decision-making at the velvet rope.

### Visual Style
A hybrid of **Glassmorphism** and **Tactile Luxury Noir**. Deep obsidian background planes layer beneath frosted smoked-glass cards, framed by brushed champagne metallic accents and activated by soft, diffuse amethyst luminescence.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in abyssal darks, lit by warm champagne metallic luster and electric violet atmospheric undertones.

### Color Roles & Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#0B0B10`):** Obsidian black canvas, absorbing glare in dimly lit club entryways.
- **Surface Elevation (`#13111C`):** Deep smoked-violet slate used for base glass containers and layered surfaces.
- **Primary / Brushed Gold (`#D4AF37`):** Precious metal tone used for active states, key counts, structural frames, and primary actions.
- **Secondary / Neon Amethyst (`#8A2BE2`):** Electric night glow dedicated to live signals, pulse animations, VIP alerts, and ambient lighting accents.
- **Tertiary / Pale Champagne (`#F5D77F`):** High-luminance gold for key statistics, selected icons, and focal highlights.
- **Deep Bronze (`#997B24`):** Subdued gold for secondary borders, disabled gold states, and segmented rules.
- **Electric Violet (`#6C28D9` / `#A855F7`):** Accent glow layers, warning thresholds, and ambient backdrops.
- **Text Primary (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast optical white for vital real-time numbers and headings.
- **Text Secondary (`#9E9AA8`):** Muted platinum-grey for metadata, capacity caps, and floor indicators.

## Typography

The editorial voice pairs classic jazz-era sophistication with contemporary operational clarity.

- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Conveys the vintage charm of high-society jazz clubs, featuring high-contrast strokes and expressive serifs. Used exclusively for venue titling, room labels, and grand summaries.
- **Numbers & Metrics (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Real-time venue headcount demands instantaneous legibility in dark environments. Large figures utilize the geometric, high-x-height sans-serif with tabular figures to avoid layout jitter during rapid incrementation.
- **Body & Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, crisp, neutral, ensuring seamless functional clarity on tablets, handheld door devices, and control consoles. Labels feature expanded tracking for refined elegance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an adaptive 12-column grid for wide executive panels and a focused single/dual-column canvas for mobile door hosts.

- **Desktop & Command Displays (12 Columns):** 2rem outer margins, 1.5rem gutters. Accommodates multi-room overviews (Main Stage, Speakeasy Bar, Mezzanine Lounge, VIP Balcony) alongside unified club totals.
- **Tablet / Bouncer Handhelds (6 Columns / Flex Dock):** Centralized tap zones optimized for single-handed thumb operation. Steppers (+ / - counters) dominate the lower 60% of the active viewport.
- **Mobile (Single Column):** 1rem margins with full-width stacked status modules and fixed floating counter controllers pinned to the bottom thumb zone.
- **Rhythm:** Metric cards prioritize high internal padding (`space-xl`) to establish luxury breathing room despite dense dynamic telemetry.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is created through layered smoked crystal, hairline gold framing, and chromatic edge bleeding.

- **Base Layer (L0):** Solid `#0B0B10` infused with an ambient, off-center radial gradient of `#8A2BE2` at 8% opacity and 120px blur to mimic distant stage lights.
- **Smoked Glass Cards (L1):** Background `rgba(19, 17, 28, 0.65)` layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(140%)`. Border is a crisp 1px hairline gradient flowing from `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.45)` at the top edge to `rgba(138, 43, 226, 0.15)` at the bottom edge.
- **Active Focus & High Capacity (L2):** Background `rgba(26, 21, 38, 0.85)` with a multi-layered drop shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`, enveloped by an external atmospheric halo of `0 0 40px rgba(138, 43, 226, 0.22)`.
- **Modals & Over-Capacity Banners (L3):** Smoked dark violet backplate bordered with solid brushed gold (`#D4AF37`) and an inner ambient glow of `inset 0 0 16px rgba(245, 215, 127, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The geometric signature combines sleek, modern curves with clean precision edges to echo mid-century cocktail lounges.

- **Primary Containers & Metric Cards:** 0.5rem (8px) base radius for inner interactive tiles, scaling to 1rem (16px) for major outer modules.
- **Steppers & Increment Triggers:** Generous 1rem (16px) roundedness to provide tactile, thumb-friendly hit targets that feel intentional and sculpted.
- **Status Pills & Live Chips:** Fully rounded capsule/pill architecture to soften the visual landscape and isolate live occupancy telemetry.

## Components

### Capacity Gauges & Stepper Buttons
- **Increment / Decrement Triggers:** Symmetrical oversized squares (minimum 64x64px on mobile) constructed of translucent obsidian (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`) enclosed by hairline brushed-gold borders. Pressing an increment button emits a localized ripple of amethyst glow (`rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.3)`).
- **Occupancy Radial Arc:** Golden brushed progress track over a dark smoked track, illuminated by a neon violet marker tip. Transitions dynamically to glowing amber at 85% capacity, and pulsing crimson/amethyst at 98%+.

### Buttons
- **Primary (Golden Velvet):** Linear gradient from `#F5D77F` through `#D4AF37` to `#997B24`. Typography in deep obsidian `#0B0B10`, bold weight. Soft ambient gold shadow (`0 4px 20px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary (Smoked Glass):** Translucent fill (`rgba(19, 17, 28, 0.7)`), 1px border of `#997B24`, text in `#F5D77F`.
- **Emergency / Lockdown:** Deep violet base with radiant amethyst border and high-visibility white text.

### Cards & Room Slices
- **Room Capacity Tile:** Smoked glass enclosure featuring the room name in Playfair Display serif, live guest number in ultra-bold sans-serif, and a bottom miniature occupancy bar with soft amethyst lighting.
- **VIP Reserved Bay:** Subtle velvet textured back-gradient, highlighted with an inset top-edge champagne highlight (`1px solid rgba(245, 215, 127, 0.6)`).

### Input Fields & Search
- Dark inset backgrounds (`#0B0B10`) with 1px border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`.
- Focus state shifts border to vibrant `#D4AF37` and applies an outer glow of `0 0 12px rgba(138, 43, 226, 0.3)`.
- Text typed appears in crisp optical white with champagne placeholder text.

### Badges & Status Indicators
- **Capacity Safe (<80%):** Pill badge in deep glass with pale gold outline and glowing text.
- **At Capacity (100%):** Pulsing violet badge with an animated neon aura and uppercase tracking.
- **VIP Waitlist:** Brushed gold foil gradient badge with dark obsidian typography.
